About Me

I'm a Junior Data Analyst with a strong passion for uncovering meaningful insights from raw, complex data. I work daily with SQL, Python (Pandas & NumPy), and Power BI to clean, analyze, and visualize data in a way that drives business decisions.

I believe that good analysis is not just about numbers, but about understanding the story behind them and translating that story into actionable recommendations for stakeholders.

I pay great attention to data quality and accuracy, ensuring that every report I deliver is reliable, consistent, and easy to interpret.

I enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams, whether it's marketing, finance, or operations, to identify trends, detect outliers, and solve real-world problems.

I'm continuously learning new tools and techniques, from advanced SQL queries to statistical analysis, to grow my skills and take on more challenging projects.
